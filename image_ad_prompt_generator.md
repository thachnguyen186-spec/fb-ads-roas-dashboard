# Image Ad Prompt Generator — 5 Styles × Any Topic

## How to use

Provide the **topic description** (niche, condition, product category, job type, etc.), then generate one prompt per style below. Each prompt produces a 1:1 square, text-heavy Facebook image ad where the **headline is the hero element**.

---

## Rules (apply to ALL prompts)

- **Headline must name the topic explicitly.** Never generic. "Enlarged Prostate" not "Health Concern." "Solar Panel Jobs" not "New Career."
- Format: **1:1 square (1080×1080)**
- Text on image: **headline + "Learn More" CTA button only.** No other text, no body copy, no subheadlines.
- **No logos, no brand marks, no real brand names, no photos of real people.**
- Headline length: **5–12 words max.**
- The image must feel native/organic — like content, not like an ad.
- Warm, muted, or earthy palettes preferred. No neon. No corporate blue-and-white.

---

## Style 1 — Notes App

**Visual concept:** Screenshot of a phone notes app or lined notebook paper. Bold black serif headline as the "note." A rounded blue button at the bottom reading "Learn More."

**Prompt template:**

> A square 1:1 social media image designed to look like a screenshot of a phone's notes app on a white lined-paper background. At the top, a simple gray toolbar with a "Notes" label. The main area shows bold black serif text that reads: "[HEADLINE]". Below the text, a rounded blue button with white text reading "Learn More". Clean, minimal, no other elements. The feel is casual, personal, like someone jotted down a genuine discovery. No logos, no brand marks.

---

## Style 2 — Retro Pixel Art / Old Computer Window

**Visual concept:** A nostalgic old-school computer dialog box or window frame (think early Mac/Windows 95 aesthetic). Warm illustrated pixel-art elements related to the topic around the edges. Bold headline inside the window. A pixelated "Learn More" button.

**Prompt template:**

> A square 1:1 social media image styled as a retro computer window or dialog box with a chunky title bar and rounded close/minimize buttons. The background inside the window is off-white or cream. Bold condensed black text centered in the window reads: "[HEADLINE]". Small warm-toned pixel-art illustrations related to [TOPIC VISUAL ELEMENT — e.g. "a house roof," "a pill bottle," "a tractor"] are placed in the corners or margins. At the bottom, a pixelated rectangular button reads "Learn More". The overall palette is warm: amber, dusty orange, cream, soft olive. Nostalgic, playful, content-native feel. No logos, no brand marks, no photographs.

## Style 3 — Editorial Illustration

**Visual concept:** A muted, warm-toned illustrated scene related to the topic fills the background. Bold condensed uppercase headline overlaid on the illustration, high contrast. "Learn More" button at bottom. Feels like a magazine cover or feature article card.

**Prompt template:**

> A square 1:1 social media image with a stylized editorial illustration filling the background. The illustration depicts [TOPIC SCENE — e.g. "a person sitting at a kitchen table looking at paperwork," "a worker on a rooftop with solar panels," "an elderly person walking in a garden"]. The color palette is muted and warm: terracotta, sage green, dusty rose, soft gold, warm gray. Overlaid on the illustration in bold condensed uppercase sans-serif text (white or cream with a subtle shadow for readability): "[HEADLINE]". A small rounded button at the bottom reads "Learn More" in a contrasting warm tone. The overall feel is editorial, thoughtful, like a feature article thumbnail. No logos, no brand marks, no photorealism.

## Style 4 — Handwritten on Craft Paper

**Visual concept:** Aged craft/brown paper or cardboard texture background. Headline in a bold hand-lettered or handwritten-style font (black ink). Simple hand-drawn doodles or underlines around the headline. A torn-paper-edge "Learn More" button or a circled CTA.

**Prompt template:**

> A square 1:1 social media image with a textured aged kraft paper or brown cardboard background. The headline is written in bold black hand-lettered style (thick brush or marker feel): "[HEADLINE]". Simple hand-drawn doodles related to [TOPIC VISUAL ELEMENT — e.g. "a small house sketch," "a stethoscope doodle," "a dollar sign"] appear near the edges or underline the text. At the bottom, a hand-drawn rounded rectangle with "Learn More" written inside in a casual script. The overall feel is authentic, personal, tactile — like a handmade sign or a page from someone's journal. Warm browns, black ink, maybe a single accent color (dusty red or deep teal). No logos, no brand marks, no photographs.

---

## Style 5 — Bold Typography on Solid Color Block

**Visual concept:** A solid warm-colored background (mustard, terracotta, deep sage, dusty navy) split into two color blocks or with a single accent stripe. Massive bold sans-serif headline dominates the frame. Minimal — just type and color. "Learn More" in a contrasting pill-shaped button.

**Prompt template:**

> A square 1:1 social media image with a clean solid background in [COLOR — e.g. "warm mustard yellow," "muted terracotta," "deep sage green"]. The entire frame is dominated by large bold sans-serif text in a contrasting color (white, cream, or black) that reads: "[HEADLINE]". The text is centered and fills at least 60% of the frame. A thin horizontal accent line or a second color block in a complementary warm tone adds subtle visual interest. At the bottom, a pill-shaped button in a contrasting color reads "Learn More". Extremely minimal — no illustrations, no photos, no decorative elements. The power is in the typography and color alone. No logos, no brand marks.
